# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique ideas and code, not people. Welcome different experience levels and backgrounds. Do not harass, threaten, demean or disclose another person's private information.

Maintain focus on useful engineering discussion. Respect maintainer decisions and accept requests to stop disruptive behavior. Report harmful conduct privately to a maintainer through an established private channel; do not publish sensitive details.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation or ban repeated offenders. They should apply these rules consistently, explain decisions where appropriate, and protect reporters' privacy.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and project community spaces. It does not claim certification under another organization's code-of-conduct program.
